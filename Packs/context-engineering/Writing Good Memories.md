# Writing Good Memories

Memories an AI can actually use are:

- **Atomic** — one fact, decision or preference per note.
- **Self-contained** — understandable without the conversation that produced it ("Prefers German for personal notes, English for code").
- **Dated and sourced** — when and where it came from, so outdated memories can be spotted.
- **Linked** — connect to the project or area it belongs to; supersede old memories instead of contradicting them silently.
- **Reviewed** — accept AI-proposed memories only if you'd be fine with every future AI session believing them.
- **Private when needed** — health, relationships and finances can stay in your vault without ever reaching an AI tool.
