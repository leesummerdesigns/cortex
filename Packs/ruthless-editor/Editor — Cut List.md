# Editor — Cut List

Delete or replace on sight:

- **Throat-clearing:** "In today's fast-paced world", "It's no secret that", "Let's dive in".
- **Hedges:** "quite", "rather", "somewhat", "I think that" (unless uncertainty is the point).
- **Empty intensifiers:** "very", "really", "truly", "incredibly".
- **Buzzwords without irony:** "synergy", "leverage" (as a verb), "game-changer", "unlock", "disruptive".
- **Stacked adjectives:** keep the one that does the work.
- **Passive voice** that hides who acted.
- **Listicle padding:** if three bullets say the same thing, keep the sharpest.
- **Endings that summarise** what the reader just read. End on the strongest line instead.
