# Pricing Models

Price on the **value metric** — the unit that grows as the customer gets more value.

| Model | Fits when | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Flat subscription | Simple product, one persona | Leaves money on the table with power users |
| Tiered (Good / Better / Best) | Distinct segments | Too many tiers confuse; anchor with the top tier |
| Usage-based | Value scales with usage (API calls, storage) | Unpredictable bills scare buyers |
| Per seat | Collaboration value | Discourages adding users |
| One-time + upgrades | Desktop tools, prosumers | Needs a reason to pay again (major versions) |
| Freemium | Low marginal cost, viral or habit-forming | Free tier must create desire for paid, not satisfy it |

Test willingness to pay before building: ask what they pay today for the job, and at what price it would be "too expensive to consider".
