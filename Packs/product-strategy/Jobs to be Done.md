# Jobs to be Done

People don't buy products; they "hire" them to make progress in a specific situation.

- **Job statement:** *When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome].*
- The competition is everything the customer could hire for the same job — including doing nothing or a spreadsheet.
- Jobs have **functional, emotional and social** dimensions. The emotional one often decides.
- **Switching interviews:** ask recent switchers about the moment they decided — what pushed them away from the old solution, what pulled them to the new one, what anxieties and habits held them back.
- Use it to: find positioning, prioritise features (does it make the job easier?), and spot underserved segments.
