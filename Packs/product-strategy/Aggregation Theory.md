# Aggregation Theory

In markets where distribution is free on the internet, power shifts from those who control supply to those who own the **user relationship** and aggregate demand.

- Aggregators have a direct relationship with users, near-zero marginal cost to serve one more user, and demand-driven multi-sided networks with decreasing acquisition costs.
- Suppliers come to the aggregator because that's where the users are — they become commoditised and modularised.
- The winning move is usually **the best user experience**, which attracts users, which attracts suppliers, which improves the experience.
- Questions to ask: Who owns the customer relationship? What becomes commoditised? Where are the marginal costs zero?
