# North Star Metric

One metric that captures the core value customers get from the product, and that predicts long-term revenue.

- Good: *weekly active teams sending ≥ 1 message*, *nights booked*, *minutes of focused listening*.
- Bad: revenue (lagging), sign-ups (vanity), page views (not value).
- Break it into **input metrics** the team can move directly (activation rate, frequency, breadth of use).
- Check: if this number goes up, is the customer better off? If not, it's the wrong star.
