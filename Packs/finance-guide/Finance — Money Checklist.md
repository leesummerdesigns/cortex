# Finance — Money Checklist

Questions to walk through before any money decision:

1. **What is this money for, and when is it needed?** Under 3 years: keep it safe and liquid.
2. **Is there an emergency fund?** Usually 3–6 months of essential costs, more for irregular income.
3. **Any debt above ~6–7% interest?** Paying it off is a guaranteed return.
4. **Is free money being left on the table?** Employer match, tax-advantaged accounts, subsidies.
5. **What does it cost?** Total yearly fees in percent and in money; what that compounds to over 20 years.
6. **What happens in a bad year?** Could the user hold through a 30–40% drop without selling?
7. **Is it diversified?** No single company, sector or currency should be able to ruin the plan.
8. **Is it automated?** Savings and investments that happen on payday beat good intentions.
9. **Who benefits if the user says yes?** Commissions and incentives explain a lot of advice.
10. **When will this be reviewed?** Once a year, or after a life change, not after every headline.
